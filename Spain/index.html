The site & the ticket structure

THE SITE — what you're seeing (one ticket, one day, each area entered once):

- ALCAZABA: the oldest part, the military fortress at the western tip. Climb the Torre de la Vela (watchtower) for a 360 panorama over the Albaicín, the city and the Sierra Nevada — the "king surveying his kingdom" view. No timed entry.

- PALACIOS NAZARÍES (Nasrid Palaces): the crescendo and the only TIMED-ENTRY area. The Court of the Myrtles, Hall of the Ambassadors, Court of the Lions, and the muqarnas "honeycomb" vaults — the carved stucco and tilework are what the whole trip is for. ~1.5 hrs.

- GENERALIFE: the summer palace and gardens up the hill — cypress avenues, the Patio de la Acequia (long water channel + arcing fountain jets), and the Water Stairway. The leafiest, coolest ground on site. No timed entry.

- PALACE OF CHARLES V: the Renaissance counterpoint inside the walls — a severe square exterior hiding a perfect circular two-story colonnaded courtyard. Free to walk through with any Alhambra access; only its two museums are separately ticketed. Stand in the center of the circle and look up.

TICKET STRUCTURE:

- "Alhambra General" (daytime / complete visit): the ticket you want — ~€22.27/adult on the official site (~€66.81 for 3). Includes Alcazaba + Nasrid Palaces (timed) + Partal + Generalife in ONE single-day visit; Charles V courtyard free. Ticket-only, NO guide.

- The Nasrid Palaces timed slot: when you buy a General ticket you must pick a specific Nasrid time, released every 30 min by limited capacity (~300 people/slot, ~6,600 tickets/day). It is STRICTLY enforced — be at the Nasrid checkpoint within your half-hour window (e.g. an 18:00 slot = present 18:00-18:30) or you forfeit the palaces with no refund (rest of ticket unaffected). It's ~20 min from the main entrance to the Nasrid door, so arrive 30+ min early. The Alcazaba and Generalife are NOT timed — do them before/after in any order.

- To get an English guide for the WHOLE site, you book a guided tour that BUNDLES the official ticket (see Private/Group options). The bare General ticket has no guide.

- NOMINATIVE TICKETS: every ticket carries a name + ID/passport number, checked at every access point. Buy under each adult's exact passport name and carry the original passport (national ID for EU citizens) — name must match or entry is refused; non-transferable.

NIGHT VISITS (separate tickets):

- Nasrid Palaces NIGHT visit — ~€12.73/adult, 22:00-23:30. Includes the illuminated Nasrid Palaces + Charles V palace + Gate of Justice. NOT the Generalife.

- Gardens/Generalife NIGHT visit — ~€8.48/adult — but it is PAUSED in June/July/August, so it does NOT run on Aug 14.

- They run concurrently anyway (both at 22:00), so you could only do one. On your date the only night option is the Nasrid Palaces night visit. High-season night visits run Tuesday-Saturday, so Friday Aug 14 qualifies.

OFFICIAL TICKET SITE (the only official one): ↗ link (Patronato de la Alhambra y Generalife). Everything else is a reseller; a reputable guided tour includes the official ticket.
