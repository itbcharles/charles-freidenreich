Each hotel was checked against its own website first, then brand pages, booking sites and regional tourism registries, then summer guest reviews. Nothing here is assumed from a star rating or from “most Spanish hotels have A/C” — every YES has a quoted source behind it. Where a hotel controls the thermostat centrally, where the specific room being booked isn't documented, or where guests have said it can't keep up in a heat wave, it's marked YES · but and the reason is in the note.
