Project 01

Digital Literacy Narrative.

Six-minute video about my history with computers — a third-grade computer-lab story, fifth-grade GPT-2 experiments, and what I do with AI in SF now. Edited in CapCut.

Video not loading? Open the MP4 directly.

What it is

A six-minute video on my history with computers. Three scenes: a third-grade computer-lab story at Avison Charter where my friend and I tried to read Amazon's page source; teaching myself GPT-2 and InferKit in fifth grade; and the AI events I help run in SF now.

Sources cited in the video

  • Allan Martin (2008) on functional vs. critical digital literacy.
  • Hargittai & Hsieh on anticipatory digital fluency.
  • Joanna Maciejewska's line about wanting AI to do laundry so we could write and make art.
  • Jensen Huang, paraphrased on capability becoming a commodity.
  • Takayoshi & Selfe on multimodal composition.

Process

Wrote the script first, then recorded. The recording stage was where most of the actual revising happened — a lot of sentences that read fine on the page didn't work out loud. Edited in CapCut over about 4.5 hours.

The Avison Charter paragraph got revised four times through the memoir-revision workshop. The version that made it into the narration is the fourth pass.
