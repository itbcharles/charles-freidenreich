VoiceToDo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

VoiceToDo is developed and operated by Charles Freidenreich, an individual developer (“I,” “me,” “Developer”). This policy explains what data VoiceToDo handles, why, where it goes, and your choices. It is written to be read, not skimmed — but here is the short version first.

The short version

  • No accounts. VoiceToDo never asks for your name, email, or any sign-in.
  • Your tasks live on your device. There is no server-side database of your content. Delete the app (or use Delete All Data in Settings) and it’s gone.
  • To work, the app sends data to two service providers, with your explicit in-app consent: your voice audio goes to ElevenLabs to be transcribed, and the text transcript goes to OpenAI to be organized into tasks. That’s the entire data flow.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, no sale or sharing of your data. Ever.

1. Data VoiceToDo handles

Voice recordings. When you tap record, audio from your microphone is (a) streamed to ElevenLabs’ transcription service and (b) saved temporarily to your device as a backup so nothing is lost if your connection drops. The on-device audio file is deleted automatically once your recording has been successfully turned into tasks. Recording only ever starts when you tap the record button, and only after you have granted microphone permission and agreed to the in-app data-sharing disclosure.

Transcripts and tasks. The text transcript of your recording is sent to OpenAI, which returns structured tasks, completions, and reminder rules. Transcripts, tasks, and reminder schedules are stored only on your device. I do not operate a database of user transcripts or tasks.

A random install identifier. The app generates a random identifier (not derived from you or your device’s hardware) that accompanies requests to my server. Its only purposes are rate limiting and abuse prevention (for example, enforcing free-tier limits). It is not linked to your identity and cannot be, because I collect no identity information.

Subscription status. If you subscribe to VoiceToDo Pro, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I receive only an anonymized entitlement signal from Apple’s StoreKit system confirming an active subscription. I never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account information.

What VoiceToDo does not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, or any analytics or behavioral data. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. How data is used

Your audio, transcripts, and tasks are used for exactly one purpose: providing the app’s features to you — transcribing your voice, organizing it into tasks, and scheduling the reminders you asked for. I do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any secondary purpose, and I do not use your data to train AI models.

3. Who receives data (service providers)

Data is shared only with the service providers necessary to operate the app, acting on my behalf:

Provider What they receive Why Their policy
ElevenLabs Voice audio Speech-to-text transcription elevenlabs.io/privacy
OpenAI Text transcript, your timezone, and titles of your open tasks (so spoken completions can be matched) Parsing transcripts into structured tasks openai.com/privacy
Apple Purchase transactions; notification delivery is handled by iOS on-device App distribution, payments, OS services apple.com/privacy
Hosting/CDN for my relay server Encrypted request traffic in transit Securely relaying requests without exposing API credentials in the app per provider

My relay server does not persist your audio or transcripts; it forwards them and discards them. ElevenLabs and OpenAI process this data under their API terms to provide their services to me; under those terms, API data is not used to train their models, though they may retain it for a limited period (on the order of up to 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring before deletion. These providers are bound by their published terms and policies to protections consistent with this policy. I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Consent

Before your first recording, the app presents a disclosure naming ElevenLabs and OpenAI, describing exactly what is sent to each, and asking for your explicit agreement. Recording is disabled until you agree. You can review or revoke this consent at any time in Settings; revoking disables recording (the rest of the app, including your existing tasks, keeps working).

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • On-device audio: deleted automatically after successful processing.
  • Transcripts, tasks, reminders: stored on your device until you delete them, use Settings → Delete All Data, or delete the app.
  • My server: no user content is stored. Transient processing only, plus short-lived operational logs (timestamps, status codes, the random install identifier — never audio or transcript content) retained briefly for abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Service providers: limited retention for abuse monitoring as described in Section 3, per their policies.

6. Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS. API credentials for service providers are held server-side and are never embedded in the app. Because there are no accounts and no content database, there is no central store of user content to breach. No system is perfectly secure, but the architecture is deliberately designed so that the most sensitive data — your recordings and tasks — stays in your hands.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: everything the app knows about you is visible in the app itself, on your device.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete All Data, or delete the app. Because I hold no server-side content, there is nothing further for me to delete on request — but you may contact me at cafreidenreich@gmail.com with any request and I will honor it.
  • Permissions: microphone and notification access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.
  • California residents: the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, grants rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing of personal information, with a right against discrimination for exercising them. VoiceToDo does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and honors these rights regardless of whether the law’s thresholds technically apply to a developer of this size. To exercise any right, email cafreidenreich@gmail.com.
  • Other jurisdictions: if your local law grants you additional rights, contact me and I will make good-faith efforts to honor them.

8. Children

VoiceToDo is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that provided personal information to me, contact cafreidenreich@gmail.com and I will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will change and the app will provide reasonable notice. Prior versions will remain available on request.
