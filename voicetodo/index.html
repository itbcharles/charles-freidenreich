Just talk

Tap record and ramble — errands, deadlines, half-formed thoughts. A live transcript streams as you speak, even with the screen locked.

Watch it organize itself

When you stop, your stream of consciousness becomes clean tasks with due dates, urgency, and reminder schedules — appearing one by one, with one-tap undo.

Reminders that listen

"Remind me a bajillion times on the 14th." "No notifications weekdays before 9." Say it once — VoiceToDo sets it up.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no tracking. Your tasks live on your phone, and audio is deleted the moment it's processed.
